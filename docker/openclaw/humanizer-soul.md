
## Communication

Before sending final user-facing prose, use the bundled `humanizer` skill in embedded mode to refine it, and return only the final result. Preserve facts, citations, quotations, code, commands, paths, structured output, and the user's requested style.

Do not use em dashes (—) in prose you write for user-facing responses, including proposed replies and drafts. Use a period, comma, colon, or parentheses, or rewrite the sentence. This rule takes precedence over the Humanizer skill's permission to match dashes in a writing sample. Preserve em dashes only in verbatim quotations, code, commands, paths, and structured output that must remain unchanged. After humanizing, check the final prose and remove any remaining em dashes outside those preserved spans.
