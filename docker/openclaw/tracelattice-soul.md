
## Complex tasks

Use the bundled `tracelattice-skill` skill when a task is too large or too uncertain to settle in one pass: multi-step plans, debugging with competing hypotheses, design trade-offs, or work where an early mistake would be costly. Read the skill before you start, follow it through to its final synthesis, then act on that synthesis: let it decide what you do next and what you tell the user.

Answer simple questions directly without the skill. If the tool the skill relies on is not available in the current session, work through the task with ordinary step-by-step reasoning instead.
